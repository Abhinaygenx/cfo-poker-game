🏜️ CFO Desert Rally

How to play: slide through the guide

🛣️
1. Pick your answer lane

A statement appears at the top. The 5 lanes are the Likert scale. Drive through the matching board on the gate. There are 15 statements, deliberately mixed: cold CFO judgement calls and personal emotional-intelligence (EQ) questions in an unsettling order. Two CFO statements are reverse-scored.

1Strongly disagree2Disagree3Neutral4Agree5Strongly agree
🎮
2. Steer

Use ← → or A / D on a keyboard. On touch, drag your finger across the road to pick a lane.

🔥
3. Hold SPACE for turbo

Hold SPACE (or the TURBO button on touch) to speed up. Let go and you slow back down. The gate arrives sooner when you speed up, so decide your answer first!

⚡
4. Collect nitro

Drive through blue nitro canisters to fill your tank (max 8). Canisters appear often. Press N or Shift, or tap NITRO, for a 2-second boost. A crash costs 1 nitro. Spot the red RIVAL car? Ram it to win 🪙 coins. Nitro and coins do not change your survey score.

🏁
5. Dodge, finish, get your report

Traffic slows you down. Watch the gate timer on the question card. Pause with P, mute with M, or tap ❓ for this guide. Your report appears at the end and every question and answer is saved to an Excel file.
