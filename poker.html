How to play

Goal. Play 10 hands. Finish with chips and a CFO-fit verdict.
Every hand
  1. Read the question. The clock (under the table) gives 30 seconds on reasoning puzzles, 40 on ethical dilemmas. Run out and you lose your stake.
  2. Set your bet. Drag the chip from 10% to 100% of your pot (100% is ALL IN). Your bet is locked when you tap a card.
  3. Tap a card. Five board members vote. Most for you: win your stake. Split: push, stake back. Most against: lose it.
Two kinds of question
5Ethical Decision Making dilemmas. Four options with varying degrees of compliance, disclosure, and governance.5Quantitative & reasoning puzzles. Test your financial logic, probability, and cognitive reflection.
Boosts
🔥3 wins in a row pays 1.5x, 6 pays 2x, and opens a Lucky Bonus slot machine.🛡Insurance: a loss costs half, a win pays 25% less.⏱+10 seconds.📣Ask the Board: removes one wrong card.

Lifelines: once each per game, one per hand, after the cards are face-up.
